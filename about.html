About Peekaboo HQ

Quieter, more reliable nursery ops

We’re building software for the people who run UK settings — owners and managers who need rotas, parent updates and billing to behave, every morning.

Magical where it helps. Practical where it counts.

Childcare days are full enough. Peekaboo HQ exists so the office isn’t also running a second job across WhatsApp, spreadsheets and sticky notes.

We focus on the jobs that stall a setting: keeping parents updated, getting ratios and cover right before open, and taking payments without an afternoon of chasing. The product should feel clear and sturdy — not loud for the sake of it.

Peekaboohq Ltd is a UK company. The app is UK hosted. We say Ofsted-ready when we mean organised records and evidence — never a claim about any setting’s Ofsted rating.
